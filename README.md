## What the app is

It watches your home without cameras. It uses your computer's Wi-Fi and, if you want, its speaker and microphone. Everything stays on your computer.

## How it works

1. You walk around with your laptop and click where you stand on a grid.
2. At each spot, the app records the Wi-Fi signals it hears.
3. From these, it builds a map of your home.
4. Later, it watches for signals that jump around. That means something is moving.
5. If you turn on sound, it also listens for echoes.

## What is built

**Map and views**

- A 3D map you can turn and zoom.
- A flat top view.
- Several floors, shown one at a time or stacked.
- Walls and furniture you place yourself. The app cannot see them.
- A button to mark where the computer sits.

**Sensing**

- Live Wi-Fi signal list.
- Likely thick walls, shown with a confidence level.
- A guess of which spot and floor the computer is on.
- Movement in the house, from Wi-Fi.
- Doors and windows: open, closed or unknown, after you train them.
- Sound: wall distances, motion, breathing, and Heart rate.

**Where is the movement?**

- An amber cloud shows the likely area.
- A bigger cloud means less certainty.
- It uses Wi-Fi links and the sound distance.
- With weak evidence, it shows a ring.

**Person or pet?**

- You tap Person, Pet or Other when movement is seen.
- After a few examples, it says "likely person" or "likely pet".
- Before that, it says unknown.

**Away mode**

- Press Arm when you leave.
- It alerts you on movement, changes, sound motion, or a door opening.
- Each alert shows its evidence.
- You can mark each alert real or false.
- It warns you if it stops working.

**Privacy and safety**

- Data is encrypted on your computer.
- The app makes no network connections.
- There is a Pause button.
- Pet-safe mode makes the sound quieter and shorter.
- There is also a quiet-hours setting.
- You can have several houses, plus backups.

## Small flats

- One router counts as one source.
- Neighbours' routers add extra directions.
- Two usable networks are enough to judge movement.


## What is to come
